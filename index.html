What this is

We opened Vincen the way a new user does, then held its paywall up against the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and this year we published what 78,000+ of their paywalls tell us. We pointed that at Vincen: installed it, went through the first screens, read your App Store listing, and compared your paywall with what the report says top apps do and what wins in A/B tests.

What follows is four ideas, each tied to a number from the report and built into a clickable prototype of your paywall and what happens when someone closes it. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Productivity apps in the US where the report cuts that finely; the A/B test results cover all apps.

We did not redesign your app

The paywall in the prototype is yours: your spiral, your serif, your emoji feature list, your blue plan card and badge, your white Continue button. We changed what it offers and what happens on close, not how it looks.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, cosmetics last: in the report, changing the trial wins 60% of A/B tests on LTV, while visual and copy changes win 35%. Each idea opens the screen that shows it.

01

Put a 7-day trial on Yearly, and say when it bills

Yearly is already pre-selected with your 35% saving and 10.000 bonus credits. What it lacks is a way in: there is no trial on either plan, and the button says “Continue”. Add a 7-day trial to Yearly only, and show the three dates under the plans: today, a reminder on day 5, the first charge on day 7.

02

Give the close button a second chance

Today the X drops people straight into the app, and the next time they see a price is when they tap Upgrade. Show one sheet first: start with a month at your own 22,99 €, no yearly commitment, compared side by side with Yearly. No new price and no invented discount.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · only 4% of apps show a second paywall after close. It converts at 0.88× the main one. See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on
Adapty data
03

Show your 4.7 stars on the paywall

Vincen holds 4.7 from 1.8K ratings on the US App Store, and the paywall never mentions it. One line under the headline puts that proof next to the price, where a new user is deciding whether an AI agent is worth 179,99 € a year.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Let the X wait three seconds

The X is live from the first frame, so a new user can dismiss the paywall before reading a line of it. Fade it in after three seconds with a small ring, the length most apps that delay it use.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · 22% of apps delay the close button; half of those wait 3–5 seconds. See the screen CR +8–15%Observed in winning
A/B tests
Potential result

All four change the offer and the moment around your paywall, not its look: a way in on Yearly, a reason to trust it, a pause before the X, and a second chance after it.
